Questions

Can I cancel any time?

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time. Yearly plans are paid up front and can be refunded in the first 14 days.

What happens if I miss a booked class?

Cancel up to 2 hours before and nothing is lost. Late cancellations count as a used class on the Starter plan.

Do I need to bring anything?

Just a water bottle and trainers. We provide mats, weights and towels.

Is there a joining fee?

No. The price you see is the price you pay.
